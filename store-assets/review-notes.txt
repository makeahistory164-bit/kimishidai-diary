2. PURPOSE OF THE APP AND TARGET USERS
--------------------------------------------------
"Kimishidai Diary" is a personal reflection journal for Japanese-speaking adults.

Problem it solves:
Many people spend their days accommodating others and gradually lose track of
what they themselves wanted. Traditional free-form journals require too much
effort to sustain, so the habit dies within days.

What the app does:
Each day the app presents 5 questions drawn from a fixed pool of 112 questions
across 10 themes (work, relationships, money, health, family, romance, career,
use of time, promises to oneself, emotions). The user answers each on a 4-point
scale and may optionally add a one-line memo. A reflection takes about 3 minutes.

From the answers the app computes an "axis score" (0-100) representing how much
the day was lived according to the user's own values. Each day on the calendar is
filled with a color derived from that score, so a month of self-consistency is
visible at a glance without re-reading any text.

Target users: adults who feel they constantly defer to others and want a
lightweight daily reflection habit that they can actually sustain.

3. HOW TO SET UP AND ACCESS THE MAIN FEATURES
--------------------------------------------------
No account, no login, no demo credentials and no sample files are required.
All free features are available immediately after installation and work offline.
The in-app purchase requires a network connection.

- On first launch, a 3-step onboarding appears. You may tap "スキップ" (Skip)
  at any time. Selecting interest themes is optional.

- Tab 1 "質問" (Questions): tap "今日の質問を始める" (Start today's questions).
  Five questions appear. For each, choose one of four options
  ("そう思わない" / "あまり思わない" / "やや思う" / "そう思う").
  The text field below each question is an optional memo.
  After answering all five, the axis score for the day is displayed.

- Tab 2 "やること" (To-dos): type a task in the field and tap "追加" (Add).
  Tap the circle on the left to mark it done; a sheet then asks who the task was
  for (yourself / family / work / others). A breakdown is shown at the bottom.

- Tab 3 "カレンダー" (Calendar): every answered day is colored by its score.
  Tap any colored day to see that day's questions and answers.
  "この月を画像で共有する" exports an image containing only the colors and the
  number of recorded days. It never includes answers or memo text.
  "この月のレポートを見る" (View this month's report) appears once the displayed
  month has a record. The monthly report requires the "プラス" (Plus) purchase.

- Tab 4 "設定" (Settings): choose interest themes, enable a daily local reminder
  notification, open the "プラス" (Plus) purchase screen, and open the Terms of Use
  and Privacy Policy.

- In-app purchase: "プラス" (Plus) is a one-time, non-consumable purchase. It unlocks
  calendar history older than the most recent 3 months and the monthly report.
  Purchase and "購入を復元" (Restore Purchase) are on the Plus screen.

Please note: the calendar fills in as the user records days over time. On a fresh
installation the calendar is empty, which is the expected initial state.

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES, TOOLS OR PLATFORMS
--------------------------------------------------
The only external services are Apple's In-App Purchase and RevenueCat, which manages
purchase status. RevenueCat receives purchase information, an anonymous app user ID
and technical device and network information.
Diary data is never sent to it.

The app does NOT use:
- any data provider or backend server of our own
- any authentication service (there are no accounts)
- any AI or LLM service
- any analytics, attribution or advertising SDK

All data (answers, memos, to-dos, settings) is stored in an SQLite database on
the device and never leaves it. Notifications are local notifications scheduled
on the device; the app does not use push notifications and never requests or
transmits a push token.
