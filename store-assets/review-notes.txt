Kimishidai Diary is a daily reflection journal for Japanese-speaking adults. The user answers 5 questions a day on a 4-point scale. The app calculates a 0-100 score and shows it as a color on a calendar.

SETUP
No account, login or demo credentials are required. On first launch, a 3-step onboarding appears. You can tap "スキップ" (Skip).

MAIN FEATURES
- Tab 1 "質問" (Questions): tap "今日の質問を始める" (Start today's questions) and answer the 5 questions.
- Tab 2 "やること" (To-dos): add a task and mark it done.
- Tab 3 "カレンダー" (Calendar): shows answered days in color. "この月を画像で共有する" exports an image with only the colors and the number of recorded days. "この月のレポートを見る" (View this month's report) appears once the displayed month has a record.
- Tab 4 "設定" (Settings): themes, local reminder, Plus purchase screen, Terms of Use and Privacy Policy.
On a fresh install the calendar is empty until the questions are answered.

IN-APP PURCHASE
"プラス" (Plus) is a one-time, non-consumable purchase. There are no subscriptions. It unlocks calendar history older than the most recent 3 months and the monthly report. Other features remain free. Open it from Settings > "プラス". Purchase and "購入を復元" (Restore Purchase) are on that screen.

EXTERNAL SERVICES
The only external services are Apple's In-App Purchase and RevenueCat, which manages purchase status. RevenueCat receives purchase information, an anonymous app user ID, and technical device and network information. Diary data is never sent to it. Answers, memos and to-dos are stored only on the device. Notifications are local. The app has no AI, analytics, advertising or backend server of our own. Free features work offline. The in-app purchase requires a network connection.

REGION
The app is distributed in Japan only, and the interface is Japanese only.

CONTENT
This app is not a medical, mental-health treatment, financial or gambling app. Its Terms of Use state that it does not provide medical or similar professional advice. All content is original work by the developer. Users cannot share content with one another through the app, other than exporting the calendar image themselves.
