Product: Plus Lifetime (kimishidai_plus_lifetime)
Type: Non-consumable, one-time purchase. Not a subscription.

What it unlocks:
- Calendar history older than the most recent 3 months
- The monthly report (a summary of the user's records for each month)
Daily questions, answers, to-dos and the most recent 3 months of the calendar stay free.
The monthly report is calculated on the device. No AI is used.

How to reach the purchase screen:
No account or login is required.
1. Tab 4 "設定" (Settings) > section "プラス" (Plus) > row "未購入（無料プラン）" (Not purchased).
Other entry points:
2. Tab 3 "カレンダー" (Calendar): tap the left arrow until you pass the free 3-month range.
3. Tab 1 "質問" (Questions): answer today's 5 questions, then open Tab 3 and tap "この月のレポートを見る" (View this month's report).

How to confirm the purchase:
1. On the purchase screen, tap "購入する" (Purchase) and complete the sandbox purchase.
2. The screen changes to "購入済みです" (Purchased), and the Settings row shows "購入済み" (Purchased).
3. In Tab 3, "この月のレポートを見る" now opens the report.

Restore: tap "購入を復元" (Restore Purchase) on the purchase screen.

The price shown comes from the App Store and may appear in the currency of the reviewer's storefront.
Purchases are handled by Apple through the RevenueCat SDK.
